From the draft spreadsheet to the dashboard OKRs

Every draft OKR from the planning spreadsheet (left) maps onto one of three dashboard OKRs (right). Nothing was dropped — the wording changed, on purpose.

Why the wording changed: displacement

The drafts target specific corners and hotspots. But these are connected systems — hold one corner and the activity just moves a block over. Measured corner-by-corner, that reads as a win. So each dashboard OKR measures its issue district-wide, where displacement can't be mistaken for progress.
