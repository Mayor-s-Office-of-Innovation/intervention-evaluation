Bus stops — who complains, who rides, what a change would cost

Look up any Muni stop. Four community-reported signals at the stop, the same signals at its neighbours and the surrounding block, and the cost side — routes, boardings, and the walk to the next stop — side by side. Complaints measure who reports, not harm; removal is one option among several reversible ones.
