Screen 1 · Where this lives

Today's hotspots view

Inside the Drugs and Unsheltered homelessness dashboards, the "Hotspots: what moved in the selected window" map sits below the chart. It classifies each ~block dot as Persistently hot, Newly emerged, or Cooled. Today, clicking a block opens a small popup anchored to the dot. All three changes build on this view.

  • 3Clicking a block opens this small popup today, anchored to the dot — the sparkline and numbers are cramped, and there's no room for more detail. Screen 4 opens it into a larger panel.
  • 1The page-level time-of-day filter (Day / Night) sits above this panel and governs the map. Screen 2 keeps it and adds a slider.
  • 2Blocks are classified persistently hot / newly emerged / cooled — there's no day-vs-night volume split yet. Screen 3 adds it.

Screen 2 · Change 1

Filter hotspots by time of day — with finer windows

Keep the easy Day/Night button for quick use, and add a time-of-day slider for any specific window. When a window is chosen, show the detail that explains why each hotspot is hot in it.

  • 1Both controls. Keep the easy Day/Night button for the common case, and add a draggable time-of-day slider to isolate any specific window (e.g. 9pm–1am). Map markers re-weight to the chosen window.
  • 1Detail that explains the hotspot. Selecting a hotspot shows its count in the window and its hourly shape — e.g. "70% between 9pm–1am, peaks ~11pm" — so the map answers when, not just where.

Screen 3 · Change 2

See hotspots by day vs. night volume

Add a Day-vs-night view so each hotspot shows its split, and you can tell at a glance which corners are daytime problems and which are nighttime ones.

  • 2A Day-vs-night toggle on the map. "Combined" is today's behavior; "Day vs night" colors each hotspot by its split instead of just including/excluding a bucket.
  • 2Split per hotspot. Each marker is sized by total volume and divided into day (amber) vs night (indigo) share; the side list ranks corners and labels them "Mostly night / Even / Mostly day," so staffing can match the time the problem actually happens.

Screen 4 · Change 3

Open the block detail into a larger, readable panel

Today, clicking a block opens a small popup anchored to the dot — the sparkline and numbers are hard to read, and there's no room for the new time-of-day and day/night detail. Open the detail into a larger docked panel beside the map instead.

  • 3Open into a docked panel, not an overlay popup. Clicking a block opens a panel beside the map with the full read — classification, district, before/now, district-tide, and a larger chart — so nothing is cramped.
  • 3This panel is where #1 and #2 live. The bigger surface gives room for the time-of-day breakdown (Change 1) and the day-vs-night split (Change 2) on the selected block — which the small popup has no space for.

Scope & decisions

What's decided

Controls (#1) — ship both. Keep the easy Day/Night button for quick use, and add a time-of-day slider for any specific window.

Block detail (#3) — open it up. Replace the small map popup with a larger docked panel beside the map; it holds the existing read plus the new time-of-day and day/night detail.

Scope — Drugs and Unsheltered homelessness only. All three changes apply to these two OKR dashboards across all four pods (Northern, Central, Mission, Tenderloin). Not applied to property & street crime, which has no hotspots map.

To confirm: the Day 6am–8pm · Night 8pm–6am thresholds carry through from today; the day-vs-night split (#2) uses them. Confirm before build if they should change.
